## Studio as an inquiry space

The fictional teaching practice combines field observation, experimental making and reflective critique.

### Sample studio sequence

1. **Observe** a situated interaction.
2. **Translate** observations into a visual or material model.
3. **Prototype** a possible alternative.
4. **Reflect** on the social and ethical implications.
