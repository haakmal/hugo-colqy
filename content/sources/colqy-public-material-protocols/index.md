## A collection of experimental interfaces

Material Protocols imagines that everyday artefacts might carry instructions for interacting with institutional systems.

{{< split src="process-sequence.svg" side="left" alt="Three phases of an experimental process" caption="Image placed to the left of text." >}}
### Observe / Prototype / Reflect

A repeated cycle of observation, intervention and reflection helps communicate the process without prescribing a single outcome.
{{< /split >}}
