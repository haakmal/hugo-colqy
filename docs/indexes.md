# Indexes, Collections and Tags

Indexes are curated or automatically generated views of Colqy content. They can support portfolios, publication lists, teaching archives or chronological journals.

## Selecting entries

Choose **one selection mode** in Sveltia:

- **Automatic:** filter eligible content by content type, collection and/or tag. An empty filter does not impose that restriction. Draft and excluded utility content are not normally included in public automatic indexes.
- **Hand-picked:** choose specific Pages, Projects, Articles and/or Books. Use this when an editorial sequence matters or a fixed showcase is needed.

The two modes are alternatives, not cumulative filters. Avoid editing obsolete selection keys manually.

## Presentation

| View | Use |
|---|---|
| List | Text-oriented entries with titles, dates and summaries. |
| Grid | Card-based presentation with optional thumbnails. Text-only entries remain legible. |
| Timeline | Chronological entries grouped by year, with an Undated group. |

The retired `gallery` view is interpreted as Grid for backward compatibility; choose Grid for new Indexes.

## Collections vs Tags

Collections are broader editorial groupings, such as `research` or `practice`. Tags provide cross-cutting descriptions such as `methods` or `publishing`. Both use stable IDs with editable display labels in **Organisation**. Keep IDs consistent: changing an identifier can affect filters and existing content.

## Example scenarios

- **Practice:** Grid of selected Projects.
- **Research:** List of Articles, Projects and Books.
- **Journal:** Timeline of dated Pages and Articles, functioning as a blog without a separate Blog type.

Test automatic inclusion when adding new published entries, and confirm manually selected entries still appear when they do not match automatic filters.
